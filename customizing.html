Settings and sections

Customizing Revon

Revon is configured in Shopify's theme editor. This guide follows the labels you see there, explains what each group controls, and shows where to place the theme's 49 addable section types. No code is required for normal store setup.

Design system

All three presets use Revon's clinical minimalist and brutalist foundation: sharp corners, direct typography, visible structure, generous whitespace, and high contrast. The presets change type, color, spacing, content order, and pacing without adding rounded cards or decorative shadows.

Using the theme editor

  1. From Shopify admin, open Online Store > Themes.
  2. Find the draft copy of Revon you want to edit and select Customize.
  3. Use the template picker at the top to switch between home, product, collection, page, blog, cart, search, and other templates.
  4. Select a section in the left panel to edit it. Drag sections or blocks to reorder them.
  5. Use Theme settings for controls shared across the whole storefront.
  6. Use the desktop and mobile preview buttons before saving.

Duplicate the theme before a large visual change. A duplicate gives you a quick rollback without affecting products, collections, pages, menus, or orders.

Theme settings

Logo and branding

  • Logo: upload a transparent SVG or PNG. Test both a wide and a compact logo.
  • Favicon: upload a square image. Shopify serves it at the small browser-tab size.

Colors

Each preset includes a light and a dark color scheme. A scheme contains background, surface, primary text, secondary text, border, button background, and button text colors. Select a scheme inside a section instead of entering the same colors repeatedly.

At the top of the Colors settings sits the Brand palette — three colors (primary, secondary, accent) that capture your brand at a glance. Each preset ships its own palette; adjust it any time.

Utility colors control sale, success, warning, error, and fallback swatch states. After changing a scheme, check body text, links, buttons, disabled controls, and form errors for readable contrast.

Typography

  • Heading font: used for every heading and italic editorial accent.
  • Body font: used for paragraphs, labels, forms, and navigation.
  • Heading controls: weight, size, text transform, and letter spacing.
  • Body controls: 14-20px size and 120-190% line height.
  • UI controls: subheading and button size, tracking, and text transform.

Fonts are selected through Shopify's font picker and hosted by Shopify. Revon uses one heading family and one body family consistently; individual sections cannot introduce unrelated typefaces.

Layout

  • Page width: adjustable from 1000px to 1600px. Every preset starts at 1440px.
  • Desktop section spacing: adjustable from 40px to 120px.
  • Mobile section spacing: adjustable from 32px to 80px.
  • Uniform section heading gaps: turn this on to keep the space between section headings and grids, cards, or content consistent. Desktop and mobile gaps are adjustable separately.
  • Container padding: separate desktop and mobile edge spacing.
  • Corner style: image, button/control, and field/select corners have separate global sliders. Button radius also covers arrows, quantity controls, popup actions, and pagination. Individual visual sections expose a radius control only when their media layout needs an exception.

For a dense catalog, reduce section spacing before reducing font size. For an editorial store, keep the 1440px width and increase spacing between major story sections. Joinery starts with 96px desktop spacing, 60px mobile spacing, and restrained 4px corners.

Product cards

  • Swap to second image: fades from the featured image to image two. Products with one image keep the featured image.
  • Brighten: increases image brightness and saturation slightly.
  • Zoom image: enlarges the featured image inside its frame.
  • Lift card: moves the complete card upward by 4px without adding a rounded surface or shadow.
  • None: keeps product-card media static.
  • Show color swatches: shows up to four color options on each card; hovering (or focusing) a swatch previews that variant's image. Needs each color variant to have its own image for the preview.
  • Badges: toggles for the automatic sale and New badges, plus the custom badge tag prefix and badge colors — see Product badges.
  • Show or hide vendor and product type.
  • Enable the quick-view button, which appears on hover or keyboard focus.
  • Show or hide custom badges and choose their background and text colors.
  • Show or hide automatic Sale and New product-card badges.

Set these options under Theme settings > Product cards. The global Animations and effects > Enable image hover zoom control can suppress image movement and image swapping across the storefront. Hover-only presentation is not required to understand or purchase a product on touch devices.

Animations and effects

  • Disable reveal effects globally when you want a fully static presentation.
  • Enable or disable smooth scrolling.
  • Choose none, fade, fade-up, or blur for section reveals.
  • Enable page transitions and choose fade, fade-up, or blur.
  • Enable or disable image hover motion.

Customers who request reduced motion receive a reduced experience automatically. Keep movement restrained on product and checkout-related screens.

Cart

  • Cart type: drawer, notification, or cart page.
  • Shipping progress: optional informational bar with an adjustable threshold and editable messages.
  • Gift wrapping: optional cart attribute with a gift-message field on the cart page. It does not add a product or fee automatically.
Shipping threshold

The progress bar is messaging only. Create the real free-shipping rate in Shopify under Settings > Shipping and delivery, using the same amount.

Social, search, and advanced controls

  • Add Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, X/Twitter, and YouTube URLs.
  • Enable or disable predictive search.
  • Show or hide breadcrumbs, the back-to-top control, and scroll progress.
  • Upload a fallback social-share image and enter a fallback meta description and X/Twitter handle.

Section library

Select Add section in a JSON template to use the library. Availability can vary by template context. Start with the smallest number of sections that communicates the offer, then add detail where customers need it.

Commerce and product discovery

  • Featured products: a configurable product grid with collection or manual product selection.
  • Product recommendations: Shopify-generated related products on product templates.
  • Recently viewed: products remembered in the customer's browser.
  • Product showcase: focused product media and supporting feature blocks.
  • Collection list: collection cards selected manually or from collections.
  • Lookbook grid: editorial product or collection browsing in an asymmetric or regular layout.
  • Shop the look: one room or outfit image with product hotspots positioned using X and Y controls.
  • Routine: ordered product or content steps for a regimen, process, or set.
  • Countdown timer: a live countdown to a sale or launch date. Enter the end date as YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM; when it ends the section hides itself or shows your message. Also available as a compact block on the product page.

Hero, media, and storytelling

  • Hero: one image-led opening section with heading, eyebrow, CTA, alignment, overlay, and responsive height controls.
  • Hero slider: multiple campaign slides with separate desktop and mobile images.
  • Image with text: image and copy in a two-column layout with optional quote, stat, or feature blocks.
  • Split banner: two image panels with independent copy, links, overlays, and alignment.
  • Editorial mosaic: configurable image tiles for magazine-like compositions.
  • Editorial statement: a large text-led brand statement.
  • Product editorial: long-form product or material story with supporting feature blocks.
  • Story carousel: sequenced cards that can connect editorial images and products.
  • Parallax image: a full-width image section with controlled scrolling movement.
  • Video feature: hosted or external video presentation with fallback imagery and text.
  • Before / after: an accessible comparison slider using two related images.
  • Clinical results: evidence-led result blocks with a source field. Use only claims you can document.
  • Key ingredients: ingredient or material cards with images, descriptions, and optional product links.
  • Features grid: repeated icon-and-text benefits or service details.
  • Stats: repeated numeric facts for verified store or product information.

Trust and engagement

  • Testimonials: customer or editorial quotes. Publish only authentic, authorized content.
  • Brand logos: authorized brand, stockist, certification, or press logos.
  • FAQ: expandable question blocks with optional CTA. Choose Centered (default) or Left — a two-column editorial layout with a sticky heading — in the section's Layout setting.
  • Blog carousel: selected blog posts with image and metadata controls.
  • Newsletter: Shopify customer signup form with configurable copy.
  • Social feed: manually managed social-style image posts; this is not a live social-network API feed.
  • Promo popup: optional timed message with image and CTA. It is disabled until you enable it.
  • Contact form: Shopify contact form plus optional email, phone, address, and hours blocks.

Utility and site sections

  • Announcement bar: one or more rotating store messages.
  • Marquee: repeating text and separators.
  • Scroll text: large text lines with optional eyebrow, description, and button blocks.
  • Apps: a dedicated wrapper for app blocks.
  • Custom Liquid: merchant-managed Liquid or approved embed code.
  • Header: logo, navigation, localization, search, account, cart access, promotional images, and editable mega-menu product cards. Add one or two Menu product blocks, enter the matching parent and column titles, then select the products to feature.
  • Footer: menus, newsletter, social links, localization, payment icons, and legal links.
  • Main page sections: product, collection, cart, search, blog, article, page, password, and 404 templates.
  • Cart notification: compact add-to-cart confirmation used when that cart mode is selected.

Product page blocks

Open a product template and select Product information. Revon currently supports these reorderable blocks:

  • Vendor, title, price, inventory, description, and benefits.
  • Variant picker, rating, and buy buttons.
  • Information row, trust badges, delivery estimate, and pickup availability.
  • Size guide, share buttons, popup, tabs, and separator.
  • Custom text, Custom Liquid, and app blocks.
  • Complete the look: hand-pick up to three companion products; shoppers tick what they want and add everything to the cart in one tap, with a live total.
  • Countdown timer: a compact "Sale ends in" bar that hides itself when the timer runs out.

The product form supports native selling plans, quantity rules, volume pricing, unit pricing, accelerated checkout, local pickup, product video, external video, and 3D product models when the store data provides them.

Product badges

Revon supports your own custom card badges (via product tags), automatic status badges (sold out, sale percentage, new), and product-page trust badges.

Full walkthrough

Open the dedicated Product badges guide for setup, styling, stacking rules, bulk editing, examples, troubleshooting, and a testing checklist.

Custom product-card badges

  1. Open a product in Shopify admin.
  2. Add a tag that starts with badge: followed by your label — for example badge:Top Seller.
  3. Save. The badge appears on that product's card everywhere it's shown.

A product can carry several badges; they stack down the card's corner. The prefix and the badge colors are under Theme settings → Product cards (leave the prefix blank to turn custom badges off).

Automatic badges

Sold out follows product availability and overrides all other badges. The sale badge shows the discount percentage whenever a compare-at price is set. The New badge appears on products tagged new. The sale and New badges each have a toggle in Theme settings → Product cards.

Trust badges

Three icon-and-text reassurance chips in the product info column — edit the Trust badges block on the product template to set each icon and label. Defaults: Secure checkout, Easy 30-day returns, Fast, tracked shipping.

Variant swatches

  1. In Shopify admin, open the product and confirm the option name and values are correct.
  2. Use Shopify's product category and option-value tools to assign a color or image swatch where available.
  3. Open the product template and keep the Variant picker block enabled.
  4. Preview available, unavailable, and sold-out combinations on desktop and mobile.

Revon reads Shopify's native swatch object first. If no native swatch exists, common color names use a built-in fallback; unknown names use the global fallback swatch color.

On product cards, up to four color swatches appear under the price when a product has a color option — hovering or focusing a swatch previews that variant's image. Toggle under Theme settings → Product cards → Show color swatches.

App blocks and embeds

Revon accepts app blocks in the dedicated Apps section, the main product section, Featured products, Clinical results, and Key ingredients. App embeds are enabled from Theme settings > App embeds in Shopify's editor.

An app is not guaranteed compatible simply because it is listed in the Shopify App Store. Prefer apps that provide theme app extensions, app blocks, or app embeds, and test their storefront and editor behavior before publishing.

See App integrations for a safe installation checklist.

Gift card page

Revon ships a dedicated template for your gift card product with gift-appropriate content: "delivered instantly by email", "never expires", a How it works explainer, and a bestsellers row.

  1. In Shopify admin → Products, open your gift card product.
  2. In the sidebar, set Theme template to gift-card.
  3. To sell multiple amounts, add one option named Amount with a variant per denomination (10 / 50 / 100 / 200), each priced to match.

When a buyer sends the card as a gift, the recipient's name and personal message appear on the gift card page they receive.

Blog & articles

Article pages include a comment form (with moderation notices when you approve comments first), comment pagination, clickable tags, and share buttons. Blog sections use Shopify's default news blog — to brand it, rename that blog's title (for example to "Journal") under Content → Blog posts → Manage blogs; the handle stays news and everything keeps working.

Advanced changes

Before editing code

Duplicate the theme and record every changed file. Direct edits can complicate future theme updates and are outside standard theme configuration support.

For a small embed or Liquid fragment, use the Custom Liquid section or product block first. Edit theme files only when a section or app cannot provide the required behavior.

Do not edit config/settings_data.json to create merchant theme styles. That file is managed by Shopify and stores live editor configuration. Developers creating a distributable preset should work in source control and validate the preset schema before deployment.
