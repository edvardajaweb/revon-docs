Theme features at a glance

  • Online Store 2.0 — sections and blocks work on every page
  • 60+ purpose-built sections including editorial, lookbook, clinical results, and gradient showcase layouts
  • Native variant swatches with image and color support
  • Product pages with 3D model, video, and external video media
  • Subscriptions via Shopify selling plans
  • Multi-currency, multi-language with country and language selectors
  • Shop Pay Installments, accelerated checkout, Follow on Shop
  • Cart discounts, cart notes, and line-level discount display
  • Unit pricing for EU and Swiss merchants
  • Multi-level header navigation and collapsible mobile menu

Support policy

All Revon license holders get free bug fixes and help with theme questions. We reply to every support request within two business days. Customization work, app integrations, and help with merchant-owned code are outside the scope of free support — we can either walk you through doing it yourself or refer you to a Shopify Partner.

Updates

Revon receives regular updates for bug fixes and new features. Before editing code, always duplicate your theme from the Shopify admin so you have a backup. When a new version ships, you can install it side-by-side and migrate your settings at your own pace.
