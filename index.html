62 sections, six headliners

Six sections worth showing off.

View all sections
Hero

Cinematic hero

A full-screen video or image with a headline that fades between two lines and a scroll arrow. The main opener for the Skincare and Maison presets.

Section reference Proof

Clinical results

Big-number proof points like "97% saw smoother skin". For beauty, wellness, and supplement brands that can back up what they claim.

Section reference Story

Key ingredients

Cards that explain each ingredient — photo, description, and an optional link to the product it's in.

Section reference Compare

Before / after

Drag-slider that wipes between two photos. For showing real transformations, not exaggerated ones.

Section reference Shop

Shop the look

Place up to eight clickable dots on a photo. Each one opens a product card with an add-to-cart button. Great for fashion and home.

Section reference Editorial

Editorial mosaic

A magazine-style grid of photos with text. The main storytelling block in the Atelier preset.
