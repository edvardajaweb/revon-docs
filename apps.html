Compatibility without guesswork

App integrations

Revon supports Shopify theme app extensions, native selling plans, app blocks, and app embeds. Because every third-party app controls its own code and release cycle, compatibility must be tested on a draft theme before publishing.

What Revon supports

  • App blocks: merchant-placeable blocks provided by a Shopify theme app extension.
  • App embeds: site-wide widgets enabled from the theme editor, commonly used for chat, analytics, loyalty launchers, and overlays.
  • Apps section: a full-width wrapper that accepts app blocks on JSON templates.
  • Custom Liquid: a controlled place for an approved embed when an app does not provide a theme app extension.
  • Native selling plans: subscription and prepaid purchase options stored through Shopify's selling-plan system.
No universal guarantee

No theme can guarantee compatibility with every app. Older apps that edit theme files, replace the product form, intercept the cart, or inject fixed-position widgets can require app-vendor support or custom integration.

Install an app safely

  1. Duplicate your current Revon draft or published theme.
  2. Read the app's installation and theme-extension requirements.
  3. Install the app, but do not publish the test theme.
  4. Enable its app embed or add its app block in the theme editor.
  5. Test the editor, storefront, product options, add to cart, cart updates, checkout handoff, customer accounts, and mobile layout.
  6. Check keyboard focus, screen-reader labels, 200% zoom, and reduced motion.
  7. Compare performance before and after installation.
  8. Remove any old code snippets before testing a replacement app.

Supported app-block placements

  • Apps section: use for standalone app content on any compatible JSON template.
  • Main product: use for ratings, product-specific widgets, forms, memberships, and other product app blocks.
  • Featured product: use for app content attached to a selected featured product.
  • Featured products: accepts app blocks within the section's supported block area.
  • Clinical results and Key ingredients: accept app blocks when an app provides suitable content for those contexts.

App embeds are not placed inside a section. Enable them through Theme settings > App embeds. The exact embed label comes from the app.

Subscriptions and selling plans

Revon's product form reads Shopify selling-plan groups and sends the selected selling-plan ID when an item is added to cart. Cart lines preserve the selling-plan allocation and associated pricing.

  1. Install a subscription app that uses Shopify's selling-plan system.
  2. Create a plan and assign it to test products.
  3. Confirm one-time and subscription options appear on the product page.
  4. Change variants and confirm the available plan and price update.
  5. Add both one-time and subscription lines to the cart.
  6. Verify plan names, prices, quantities, and checkout behavior.

Use the app's block or embed for app-specific account portals, bundle builders, retention tools, or advanced subscription messaging.

Reviews and ratings

Place review widgets through an app block on the product template or through the Apps section. Revon's native rating block can display compatible review-rating metafield data when the installed app writes to Shopify's standard review metafields.

  • Test products with no reviews, one review, and many reviews.
  • Verify the rating link reaches the full review content.
  • Do not duplicate star ratings by enabling both native and app widgets in the same position.
  • Check that reviews do not overflow cards or product information at mobile widths.
  • Publish only authentic reviews and follow the app's moderation rules.

Email, SMS, and marketing forms

Revon's Newsletter section submits a Shopify customer signup form. Whether subscribers sync to an email or SMS service depends on that service's Shopify integration and configuration.

  • Use the built-in Newsletter for a simple Shopify customer signup.
  • Use an app block or app embed for multi-step forms, SMS consent, segmentation, or advanced popup behavior.
  • Do not show two signup popups at the same time.
  • Test consent text, success messages, validation, keyboard use, and localization.
  • Confirm subscribers arrive in the intended list or segment before launch.

Loyalty, referrals, and wishlists

These features are not built into Revon. Install an app that provides an app block or embed and follow the app vendor's setup guide.

  • Use app embeds for site-wide launchers only when they do not cover Revon's cart, search, localization, or back-to-top controls.
  • Use product app blocks for points, referral prompts, membership pricing, or wishlist controls.
  • Test logged-out and logged-in customer states.
  • Verify wishlist buttons do not resize product cards or hide quick view.
  • Verify account links work with the customer-account type configured for the store.

Chat, helpdesk, and search

Chat and helpdesk launchers are normally app embeds. Advanced search apps may replace predictive search or the search results template.

  • Check fixed launchers against the cart drawer, mobile menu, cookie-consent UI, and back-to-top button.
  • Ensure the launcher remains keyboard reachable and does not cover checkout CTAs.
  • For advanced search, decide whether to keep Revon's predictive search enabled.
  • Test products, collections, pages, and articles with zero and many results.
  • Shopify Search & Discovery can improve filters, recommendations, and search behavior without replacing the theme's visual interface.

Cart, upsell, bundle, and checkout apps

Cart and bundle apps have the highest integration risk because they can modify line-item properties, selling plans, discounts, quantities, or drawer behavior.

  • Test drawer, notification, and page cart modes separately.
  • Test bundles, gifts, upsells, subscriptions, and discount combinations.
  • Verify line-item properties and selling plans survive cart updates.
  • Confirm cart totals match checkout totals.
  • Confirm app-added controls remain usable with keyboard and touch.
  • Ask the app vendor for support when its script replaces or patches theme markup.

Troubleshooting an app

  1. Confirm the app is enabled on the same theme you are previewing.
  2. Check Theme settings > App embeds.
  3. Check whether its app block was added to the intended template.
  4. Preview an eligible product, customer, market, or plan.
  5. Temporarily disable other app embeds to identify conflicts.
  6. Reproduce the issue on a clean duplicate of Revon.
  7. Contact the app vendor first for errors originating from its scripts.

When contacting Revon support, include the app name, app version if visible, store preview URL, affected template, browser, screenshots, and exact reproduction steps.
