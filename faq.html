Frequently asked questions

These answers describe Revon v1.0.0 and the current Pure, Atelier, and Maison presets. Shopify admin labels can move over time, but the theme controls named here match the current theme schema.

Installation and updates

How do I install Revon?

Choose Pure, Atelier, or Maison from Revon's Shopify Theme Store listing and select Try theme. Shopify adds that preset as a draft theme. Open Online Store > Themes, find the draft, and select Customize.

Do the demo products and images come with the theme?

No. Products, collections, pages, menus, blog posts, and demo-store imagery are store data and are not transferred. The installed preset provides its template layout, settings, editable starter copy, and empty resource or image pickers.

How do Shopify Theme Store updates work?

When an update is available, Shopify can add the updated version to your draft themes. Theme-editor customizations are normally copied into the updated version. Compatible code edits can also be included, while conflicting edits require review. Always preview the updated draft before publishing and keep the previous version as a fallback.

Will products or orders be deleted when I switch themes?

No. Products, collections, customers, orders, pages, and menus are managed separately in Shopify admin. Theme layouts, section settings, wording, app placements, and code changes belong to each theme copy and should be reviewed before publishing a different theme.

Presets and theme styles

Which preset is the default?

Pure is the root and default preset. It is designed for beauty, wellness, personal-care, supplement, and fragrance catalogs.

What is different about Atelier and Maison?

Atelier installs fashion-focused homepage, product, About, header, and footer files with Cormorant typography. Maison installs furniture and interiors-focused files with Noto Serif typography. Both use the same core section library and commerce code as Pure.

Does changing Theme style replace my homepage?

No. Theme settings > Theme style applies the selected style's global settings, including fonts, color schemes, spacing, motion, and cart type. It does not replace an already-customized section layout with another preset's full homepage. Duplicate the theme before changing style.

Can I use a Pure section in Maison or Atelier?

Yes. The addable section library is shared. For example, Maison can use Before / after, Atelier can use Key ingredients, and Pure can use Editorial mosaic. Check that the section fits the products and keep claims and imagery authentic.

Design, images, and content

Can I change the 1440px page width?

Yes. Open Theme settings > Layout. Page width is adjustable from 1000px to 1600px in 10px steps. All three presets start at 1440px.

Can I add rounded corners?

Revon's built-in design system uses sharp corners across all three presets. There is no global radius control. Adding rounded corners requires custom CSS and changes the intended brutalist visual language.

Can I use AI-generated images?

Yes, when the generator's terms allow commercial use and you have rights to every input. Keep prompts, original outputs, generation dates, and the service terms. Avoid logos, celebrities, copied designer products, misleading text, watermarks, and recognizable third-party intellectual property. Inspect AI images for impossible product construction and inconsistent details.

What image sizes should I upload?

Upload the largest clean source available and keep a consistent aspect ratio for each use. Product imagery commonly works well at 2000px or more on the longest edge. Wide hero images should have enough resolution for large desktop displays and a separate mobile image when the section provides one. Shopify generates responsive renditions automatically.

How do I avoid unsupported claims?

Use Clinical results and Stats only for information you can document. Use real prices and discounts, authentic reviews, authorized logos, accurate shipping language, and policies that match your business. Keep a source for percentages, certifications, awards, test results, and press quotes.

Sections and customization

How many addable sections does Revon include?

The current theme schemas expose 39 addable section types, including Apps, Custom Liquid, commerce, storytelling, navigation, and engagement sections. Template-specific main sections are also included for product, collection, cart, search, blog, article, page, password, and 404 views.

How does Shop the look work?

Upload one lifestyle image, then add Product blocks. Each block selects a product and provides X and Y position controls for the hotspot. Preview hotspots on desktop and mobile and keep them away from image edges and important product details.

How should I use Before / after?

Upload two closely matched images with the same dimensions, crop, angle, lighting, and scale. Write accurate labels and alternative text. For beauty or wellness results, obtain consent and keep evidence for any accompanying claim.

Does the Social feed connect to Instagram or TikTok?

No. Social feed is a manually managed image section and does not call a social-network API. Upload authorized images and add links yourself. Use an app block or embed if you need a live third-party feed.

Where should I add custom code?

Use a Custom Liquid section or product block for a small approved embed. For app integrations, prefer app blocks and app embeds. Duplicate the theme before editing Liquid, CSS, JavaScript, templates, or configuration files directly.

Products, variants, and cart

Does Revon support subscriptions?

Revon supports Shopify's native selling plans. Use a subscription app that stores plans through Shopify, assign a plan to test products, and verify one-time and subscription lines in the product form, cart, and checkout.

Does Revon support video and 3D product media?

Yes. The product gallery supports images, hosted video, external video, and Shopify product-model media. Add the media to the product in Shopify admin and test loading, controls, variant media changes, mobile framing, and model interaction.

How do native color swatches work?

Assign a Shopify product category, connect the relevant category metafield to the variant option, and use entries that provide a color or image swatch. Revon reads Shopify's swatch object. Common color names have a fallback, and unknown names use the global fallback swatch color.

Does the gift-wrapping setting charge a fee?

No. The setting displays a gift-wrap checkbox and message field on the cart page and saves them as cart attributes. It does not add a gift-wrap product or price. Use an app or a real product workflow when gift wrapping must add a charge.

Does the free-shipping bar create a shipping rate?

No. It is an informational progress bar. Create the actual rate under Settings > Shipping and delivery and enter the same threshold in Revon's Cart settings.

Which cart modes are available?

Drawer, notification, and page. The full cart page remains available at /cart. Test the selected mode with discounts, selling plans, quantities, line-item properties, shipping messages, and accelerated checkout.

Apps and privacy

Does Revon work with every Shopify app?

No theme can guarantee every app. Revon supports app blocks, app embeds, a dedicated Apps section, Custom Liquid, and native selling plans. Test each app on a duplicate theme and contact the app vendor when its code replaces product or cart markup.

Where can app blocks be placed?

Revon accepts app blocks in the Apps section, Main product, Featured product, Featured products, Clinical results, and Key ingredients. App embeds are enabled separately from the theme editor's App embeds panel.

Does Revon include a cookie-consent banner?

No. Use Shopify's customer privacy settings and consent tooling, or a compliant consent app suited to the markets where you sell. Do not use a decorative theme popup as a substitute for consent management.

Markets, currencies, and languages

Which language files are included?

English is the default, with German, Spanish, and French locale files included. Review every translation for your store's products, policies, and terminology. Use Shopify Translate & Adapt, CSV translation, or another translation workflow for additional languages and store content.

Does Revon support country and language selectors?

Yes. Enable the selectors in the supported Header or Footer settings after configuring Shopify Markets and published languages. Currency, domain, availability, pricing, and final checkout behavior are controlled by Shopify Markets and store configuration.

Performance and accessibility

What affects storefront performance most?

Large images, autoplay media, many sections, third-party app scripts, custom code, and font choices can all affect performance. Compress images, use responsive media, remove unused apps, and test Lighthouse on real home, product, collection, cart, and search pages.

Can animations be disabled?

Yes. Theme settings include global animation, reveal-effect, smooth-scroll, page-transition, and image-hover controls. Revon also responds to the customer's reduced-motion preference.

What should I accessibility-test before publishing?

Test keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader names, form labels, error messages, dialogs, drawers, media controls, color contrast, touch targets, reduced motion, and 200% zoom. Re-test after adding apps or custom code because they can change the accessibility of the final storefront.

Still need help?

Send a request through Contact support. Include the store preview URL, Revon version, affected page, browser, screenshots, and exact steps to reproduce.
