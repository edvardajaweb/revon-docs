Quick recipes

Cheat sheet

The most common tasks in Revon, each as a short recipe. Save this page or print it — most things you'll want to do are two or three clicks away.

Basics

Change your logo

  1. Open Theme settings → Logo and branding.
  2. Click the Logo image picker and upload your logo. SVG works best; PNG is fine if transparent.
  3. Save, then check the header and mobile menu at both preview sizes.

Change your favicon

  1. Open Theme settings → Logo and branding → Favicon.
  2. Upload a square image, at least 32×32 (PNG recommended).
  3. Save. Browsers will show it in the tab.

Change the colors of the whole theme

  1. Open Theme settings → Colors.
  2. Edit any color in the scheme — background, text, button background, button text, border, etc. Every section that uses this scheme updates automatically.
  3. Save.

Change the heading or body font

  1. Open Theme settings → Typography.
  2. Click Heading font or Body font and choose from Shopify's font picker.
  3. Save. Bold and italic variants load automatically.

Homepage

Add a section to the homepage

  1. Open the home page in the theme editor.
  2. Click Add section wherever you want it to appear in the stack.
  3. Pick the section type, fill in its settings, and save.

Hide a section temporarily

  1. Click the section in the theme editor.
  2. Click the eye icon at the top-right of its settings panel to hide it.
  3. Save. The section stays in the template but won't appear on the live site until you toggle it back on.

Reorder sections

  1. In the theme editor, click and hold the drag handle (six dots) next to any section in the left panel.
  2. Drag it up or down to its new spot.
  3. Save.

Change the announcement bar text

  1. In the theme editor, scroll to the top of the section list and click the Announcement bar section.
  2. Edit the message blocks and remove any that you do not need.
  3. Save.

Product page

Set up variant color swatches

  1. Assign the product's category and connect the relevant category metafield to the variant option.
  2. Choose category entries that provide a color or image swatch for each option value.
  3. Save and preview the product page's Variant picker. Revon reads Shopify's native swatch data automatically.

Configure product-card badges

  1. Open Theme settings > Product cards > Badges.
  2. Enable Sale for products with a real compare-at price and New for products tagged exactly new or New.
  3. Save and preview available, discounted, new, and sold-out products. A card shows one badge with Sold out, Sale, then New priority.

Add a product-page image or trust badge

  1. Open the product template and select Product information.
  2. Use Image badge text for a label on the main gallery image, or add the Trust badges block for up to three icon-and-text statements.
  3. Save and check every product assigned to that template because these badge settings are shared by the template.

See Product badges for priority, scope, and custom-label limitations.

Set the focal point on a product image

  1. In Shopify admin, open any product and click an image.
  2. Click Edit, then drag the focal-point indicator to the part of the image you want kept in view when cropped.
  3. Save. Revon respects this on every responsive image.

Add a 3D model or video to a product

  1. Open the product in Shopify admin.
  2. Click Add media and upload a .glb file (3D model), a .mp4 video, or paste a YouTube/Vimeo URL.
  3. Save. Revon shows it in the product gallery alongside images.

Cart & checkout

Choose the cart behavior

  1. Open Theme settings → Cart.
  2. Pick Drawer, Notification, or Page. The full cart remains available at /cart.
  3. Save.

Set a free-shipping threshold

  1. Open Theme settings → Cart and enable the shipping-progress bar.
  2. Enter the order total above which shipping is free (in your store currency, before tax).
  3. Save. The cart shows a progress bar towards the threshold.
Heads up

The progress bar is just visual. Actual free shipping is set in Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and delivery as a rate.

Navigation

Edit the main menu and dropdowns

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Content → Menus.
  2. Click Main menu and add or edit items.
  3. Drag sub-items under a parent to create dropdown navigation, then test desktop and mobile.

Show a language or country selector

  1. Enable extra languages or currencies under Shopify Admin → Settings → Languages or Settings → Markets.
  2. Open the Footer section in the theme editor.
  3. Toggle on Show language selector or Show country selector. Save.

Style & brand

Switch to a different preset

  1. Duplicate the theme so you have a rollback.
  2. In the theme editor, click Theme settings (top-left).
  3. Open Theme style, pick Pure, Atelier, or Maison, and choose Change style.
  4. Save and review every template. Theme style changes global settings, not your existing section layout.

Add social media links to the footer

  1. Open Theme settings → Social media.
  2. Paste the full URL of each profile (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, X, YouTube).
  3. Save. Icons appear in the footer for any field you filled in.

Change the global page width

  1. Open Theme settings → Layout.
  2. Adjust Page width between 1000px and 1600px. The default is 1440px.
  3. Save and check long headings, product grids, and mobile edge spacing.
Cookie consent

Revon does not include a decorative cookie banner. Configure consent through Shopify's Customer privacy settings or a suitable consent app for your markets.

Turn off all scroll animations

  1. Open Theme settings → Animations and effects.
  2. Toggle off Enable animations.
  3. Save. The theme also respects prefers-reduced-motion automatically for individual visitors.
