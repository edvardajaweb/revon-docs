Getting started

This guide walks you through installing Revon and configuring it for the first time. Plan on about 15 minutes from install to a fully customized home page.

1. Install the theme

  1. Buy Revon from the Shopify Theme Store. It will be added to your store library automatically.
  2. Go to Online Store → Themes in your Shopify admin.
  3. In the Theme library section, find Revon and click Add.
  4. Click Customize to open the theme editor.

Before you go live: Always duplicate your current published theme first. Go to Themes, click the three-dot menu on your live theme, and choose Duplicate. This gives you a rollback if anything goes wrong.

2. Set up essentials

Logo and favicon

  1. Open Theme settings → Logo.
  2. Upload your main logo. SVG is preferred; PNG works well if transparent.
  3. Upload a favicon under Theme settings → Favicon. Recommended 64×64px PNG.

Colors and typography

  1. Open Theme settings → Colors. Revon ships with a warm editorial palette you can override.
  2. Open Theme settings → Typography to change heading and body fonts. All Google Fonts and Shopify-hosted fonts are supported.

Navigation menus

  1. Go to Online Store → Navigation.
  2. Make sure a menu called Main menu exists — Revon uses it by default.
  3. For multi-level dropdowns, drop sub-items onto a parent item to nest them. Revon renders up to two levels.
  4. Create a Footer menu the same way; the footer section pulls from it by default.

3. Build your home page

In the theme editor, open the home page template. You'll see a stack of sections that come with the preset. Click any section to open its settings, click Add section to add a new one, or drag to reorder.

Recommended home-page structure

  1. Announcement bar (top) — shipping message or promo
  2. Header
  3. Cinematic hero or slideshow — primary above-the-fold moment
  4. Featured collections — 2–3 tile grid
  5. Featured products — a hand-picked row
  6. Image with text — brand story
  7. Editorial scroll or key ingredients — deeper product story
  8. Testimonials
  9. Newsletter
  10. Footer

4. Configure product pages

  1. Open a product in the theme editor via Product pages in the template picker.
  2. Each piece of the product info column is a block — drag to reorder, or remove anything you don't need.
  3. To display product variants as colored swatches, add a color metafield definition on the variant and pick either a hex or an image for each option value.
  4. To show a 3D model, upload a .glb file to your product's media. Revon will render it with the Shopify model viewer automatically.

5. Cart and checkout

Revon supports both cart drawer and cart page modes. Choose which under Theme settings → Cart. Both modes include:

  • Automatic discount code display
  • Order notes
  • Free-shipping progress bar
  • Unit price display for regulated regions
  • Accelerated checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay)

6. Go live

  1. Preview your theme thoroughly on desktop and mobile.
  2. Click Publish from the theme editor or the Themes page.
  3. Check the live site in an incognito window to confirm everything renders without your admin session.

Stuck? Head to the FAQ or contact support. We reply within two business days.
