Merchandising guide

Product badges

Highlight bestsellers, new arrivals, limited editions — any label you like — right on your product cards. Badges in Revon are controlled with product tags and a few theme settings, so there's nothing to install and no code to touch.

The short version

Add a product tag that starts with badge: — for example badge:Top Seller — and a "Top Seller" badge appears on that product's card everywhere it's shown. That's the whole system.

Badge types

Revon shows three kinds of badges on product cards, plus trust badges on the product page:

  • Automatic status badges — Sold out appears whenever a product has no available variants; the sale badge shows the exact discount (for example -20%) whenever a compare-at price is higher than the price.
  • Custom badges — your own labels, added per product with a tag. Unlimited label wording, consistent brand styling.
  • "New" badge — a convenience badge that appears on any product tagged new.
  • Trust badges — three reassurance chips (checkout, returns, shipping) in the product page's info column. These are a product-page block, not a card badge — see Trust badges.

Custom badges with tags

  1. In Shopify admin, open Products and select a product.
  2. In the Tags field, add a tag that starts with the badge prefix (default badge:) followed by your label:
    • badge:Top Seller → shows Top Seller
    • badge:Limited → shows Limited
    • badge:Award Winner → shows Award Winner
  3. Save. The badge appears on that product's card storewide — collections, search results, featured rows, recommendations, and recently viewed.

Everything after the prefix becomes the label, in exactly the capitalization you typed. A product can carry several badges at once — they stack neatly down the top-left corner of the card. To remove a badge, just delete the tag.

Good label examples

  • Merchandising: badge:Top Seller, badge:Back in Stock, badge:Last Chance
  • Product facts: badge:Vegan, badge:Fragrance-Free, badge:Handmade
  • Editions: badge:Limited, badge:Exclusive, badge:New Formula

Keep labels short — two or three words read best on narrow mobile cards.

Bulk editing

Because badges are tags, everything Shopify offers for tags works for badges: select several products in the Products list and use More actions → Add tags, or edit tags in the bulk editor. You can also build an automated collection from a badge tag — for example, a "Bestsellers" collection whose condition is tag equals badge:Top Seller — so your badge and your collection always agree.

Styling and settings

Open Theme settings → Product cards:

  • Custom badge tag prefix — defaults to badge:. Change it to anything (for example label:), or leave it blank to turn custom badges off entirely.
  • Custom badge background and Custom badge text — one brand-consistent color pair used by every custom badge.
  • Show sale badge — toggles the automatic percentage badge.
  • Show "New" badge — toggles the new-tag badge.

Appearance and rules

  • Badges sit in the top-left corner of the card image and stack vertically when a product has more than one.
  • Sold out overrides everything — when a product is unavailable, only the Sold out badge shows.
  • Order within the stack: sale percentage first, then your custom badges, then New.
  • Badge text is displayed exactly as typed (safely escaped), in the theme's uppercase label style.
  • Badge tags are ordinary Shopify tags — they don't interfere with search, filtering, or apps, and they never render as visible tag lists in Revon's cards.

Trust badges (product page)

Trust badges are the three reassurance chips in the product info column — separate from card badges. Edit them on the product template: select the Trust badges block, then set each item's icon and text. The defaults are Secure checkout, Easy 30-day returns, and Fast, tracked shipping — replace them with whatever your store genuinely offers.

Troubleshooting

The badge doesn't appear

  • Check the tag starts with your exact prefix — badge: by default, including the colon.
  • Check Theme settings → Product cards → Custom badge tag prefix isn't blank (blank disables custom badges).
  • If the product is sold out, only the Sold out badge shows — that's by design.

The badge shows the wrong text

The label is everything after the prefix, verbatim. badge: Top Seller (with a space) and badge:Top Seller both work — leading spaces are trimmed.

I want different colors per badge

Custom badges intentionally share one color pair so cards stay calm and consistent. The automatic sale badge keeps its own accent color.

Badge testing checklist

  • Add badge:Test to one product and confirm it appears on the collection grid, search results, and any featured rows.
  • Stack two badges on one product and check the layout on a narrow mobile screen.
  • Mark a product's compare-at price higher than its price and confirm the percentage badge.
  • Set a product to sold out and confirm only the Sold out badge shows.
  • Verify every badge claim against real product data and policies.
