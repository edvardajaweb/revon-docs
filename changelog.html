v1.0.0
2026

Initial theme release

Three preset install states

  • Pure: default clinical preset for beauty, wellness, personal care, supplements, and fragrance.
  • Atelier: editorial preset for fashion, jewelry, accessories, and leather goods.
  • Maison: spatial preset for furniture, home goods, lighting, ceramics, and interiors.
  • Each preset includes its own homepage, product, About, header, and footer install state.

Design and editor

  • Sharp-corner clinical minimalist design system shared across all presets.
  • Two color schemes per preset plus global utility colors.
  • Four Shopify font pickers and global heading, body, subheading, and button controls.
  • Adjustable page width, section spacing, and desktop/mobile container padding.
  • Product-card display and hover controls.
  • Configurable reveal, smooth-scroll, page-transition, and image-hover effects.

Section and template system

  • 39 addable section types across commerce, editorial, proof, engagement, and utility categories.
  • JSON templates for home, product, collection, cart, search, pages, blogs, articles, password, and 404 routes.
  • Reusable header and footer section groups.
  • Custom Liquid, app blocks, and a dedicated Apps section.

Product merchandising

  • Reorderable product identity, price, inventory, description, option, buying, pickup, trust, delivery, popup, tab, custom, and app blocks.
  • Native Shopify swatches with fallback color handling.
  • Quantity rules, volume pricing, unit pricing, selling plans, local pickup, and accelerated checkout support.
  • Product images, hosted video, external video, and 3D model media.
  • Product recommendations and recently viewed products.

Cart and discovery

  • Drawer, notification, and page cart modes.
  • Discount allocations, line-item properties, selling plans, quantity updates, and unit prices.
  • Optional shipping-progress messaging.
  • Optional gift-wrap and gift-message cart attributes.
  • Predictive search plus full product, article, page, and collection search experiences.

International and platform support

  • English, German, Spanish, and French locale files.
  • Country and language localization selectors.
  • Open Graph, social-share, canonical, and structured-data foundations.
  • Keyboard-oriented dialogs and drawers, focus styles, reduced-motion handling, and responsive layouts.
  • Shopify theme app extensions in documented supported sections.
