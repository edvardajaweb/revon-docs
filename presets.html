Docs Presets

Three focused install states

Pure, Atelier, and Maison

Every Revon preset uses the same reliable commerce foundation and sharp-corner design system. The difference is art direction: each preset supplies its own global style, homepage, product page, About page, header, footer, and industry-focused starting content.

How presets work

When installing from the Shopify Theme Store: choose the preset whose listing and demo best match your store. Shopify installs that preset's full template state, including its section order and preset-specific page layouts.

After installation: the theme editor's Theme style control changes global settings such as colors, fonts, spacing, motion, and cart style. It does not replace a homepage you have already edited with another preset's full section stack.

Choose early

Select the closest preset before adding images and rewriting section content. If you want to compare styles later, duplicate the theme first and test the change on the duplicate.

Pure

Aa / Direct, clinical, exact.

Best for: beauty, skincare, personal care, wellness, supplements, and fragrance.

Art direction: high-contrast Manrope, uppercase display typography, pure white surfaces, strong black controls, strict alignment, and evidence-led merchandising.

Global style

  • Manrope heading at weight 800, uppercase, 48px base size, and -3 tracking.
  • Manrope body at 16px.
  • 1440px page width, 96px desktop spacing, and 60px mobile spacing.
  • Fade-up reveals, page transitions, image hover, and scroll progress enabled.
  • Drawer cart by default.

The product template adds product editorial, product recommendations, a secondary featured-products edit, and recently viewed products. The About template uses a parallax banner, origin story, values, verified stats, and process content.

Atelier

A measured way of dressing.

Best for: fashion, slow fashion, jewelry, accessories, leather goods, and capsule wardrobes.

Art direction: editorial Cormorant headings, paper-grey surfaces, restrained movement, seasonal campaigns, and image-led product narratives.

Global style

  • Cormorant heading at weight 400, uppercase, 64px base size, and -1 tracking.
  • Manrope body at 16px.
  • 1440px page width, 120px desktop spacing, and 72px mobile spacing.
  • Fade reveals and page transitions enabled; scroll progress disabled.
  • Drawer cart by default.

Maison

A room with quiet structure.

Best for: furniture, home goods, interiors, ceramics, lighting, objects, and scent.

Art direction: Noto Serif headings, mixed-case editorial rhythm, cool sage surfaces, room-level merchandising, and disciplined whitespace.

Global style

  • Noto Serif heading at weight 400, mixed case, 56px base size, and neutral tracking.
  • Manrope body at 16px and 160% line height.
  • 1440px page width, 120px desktop spacing, and 72px mobile spacing.
  • Compact 12px subheadings and 11px uppercase buttons.
  • Fade reveals and page transitions enabled; scroll progress disabled.
  • Drawer cart by default.

Install the right preset

  1. On Revon's Theme Store page, compare the preset listings and open each demo store.
  2. Select Try theme from the preset you want. Shopify installs that preset's matching files into your draft themes.
  3. Open the draft in the theme editor and confirm the preset name and section layout before adding content.
  4. Keep the theme unpublished while you configure products, menus, images, policies, and apps.

Pure is the root and default preset. Atelier and Maison include preset-level listing files so their clean installs match their own demos rather than inheriting Pure's homepage.

Change theme style after installation

  1. Duplicate the theme.
  2. Open the duplicate in the theme editor.
  3. Select Theme settings > Theme style.
  4. Choose Pure, Atelier, or Maison, then select Change style.
  5. Save and review every template.
What changes

Theme style changes global preset settings such as fonts, color schemes, spacing, motion, and cart choice. Your existing section layout and section content stay associated with the current theme. Add, remove, or reorder sections manually if you want the full layout of another preset.

Replace the starter content

  • Upload your own licensed product and lifestyle images. Demo-store images are not transferred with the theme.
  • Connect every product, collection, page, blog, and menu picker.
  • Replace onboarding text, sample links, and policy prompts.
  • Use only real discounts, verified claims, authentic reviews, and authorized logos.
  • Check image crops, headings, buttons, and hotspots on desktop and mobile.
  • Keep disabled sections disabled until their content is complete.
