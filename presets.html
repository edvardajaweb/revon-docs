Docs Presets

Three voices, one theme

Presets.

Revon comes with three ready-made styles — Pure, Atelier, and Maison. Each one is a complete look: fonts, colors, spacing, animations, and a finished homepage. Pick the one closest to your brand and you're already most of the way there. Tweak from anywhere.

What is a preset?

Think of a preset as a head-start. Instead of starting with a blank theme and tuning every setting yourself, you pick a preset and Revon fills in the fonts, colors, spacing, animations, and a homepage that all work together. You can change anything afterwards — it's a starting point, not a lock.

Why three?

A beauty brand, a fashion label, and a homewares boutique each have a different visual language. Most themes make you translate yours from scratch. Revon's three presets cover those three voices, so you don't have to.

Pure

Aa — Bold & clinical.

Best for: Beauty, wellness, supplements, and fragrance brands.

How it feels: Confident and clinical. Bold uppercase headings, a warm cream background, plenty of white space. Built so that ingredient stories and proof points feel earned, not exaggerated.

Typography

  • Headings: Manrope extrabold (800), uppercase, tight letter-spacing (−3)
  • Body: Manrope regular, 16px, generous line-height
  • Display size: 48px base, scales up to 96px on the hero

Color palette

Motion & UI

  • Loading bar enabled (subtle progress indicator on page transitions)
  • Scroll progress indicator on long pages
  • Fade-up reveal animation on scroll
  • Cart drawer (not page) by default

Signature sections

  • Clinical results — stat-driven proof points (97% / 89% / 92%)
  • Key ingredients — annotated ingredient cards with imagery
  • Before/after — drag-slider for transformation imagery
  • Routine — multi-step routine builder

Who it suits: Independent skincare lines, cosmetic brands, supplement merchants, and fragrance houses that lead with ingredients or test results.

Atelier

A quiet way of dressing.

Best for: Slow fashion, capsule wardrobes, leather goods, and jewelry brands.

How it feels: Quiet and considered. Elegant serif headings, a soft paper-grey background, lots of breathing room. The opposite of fast fashion's loud saturation.

Typography

  • Headings: Cormorant Garamond regular (400), uppercase, looser tracking (−1)
  • Body: Manrope regular, 16px
  • Display size: 64px base — bigger but lighter than Pure

Who it suits: Independent fashion labels, capsule-wardrobe brands, leather and tailoring studios, fine jewelry, and accessories sold on craft.

Maison

A room that breathes.

Best for: Home goods, interiors, ceramics, scent, and lifestyle brands.

How it feels: Warm and lived-in. Refined serif body type that reads like a print magazine, a calm sage-cream palette, gently rounded corners. Built for tactile products where both the object and the room around it matter.

Typography

  • Headings: Noto Serif regular (400), mixed case (not uppercase)
  • Body: Manrope regular, 16px, 160% line-height

  • Display size: 56px base

Who it suits: Home goods boutiques, ceramics studios, candle and fragrance houses, interior accessories, and slow-living brands.

Which one to choose

If you're not sure, this is a quick decision tree:

  • You sell things people put on or in their body (skincare, cosmetics, supplements, fragrance) — start with Pure.
  • You sell things people wear (clothing, jewelry, accessories) — start with Atelier.
  • You sell things people put in their home (homewares, ceramics, candles, interiors) — start with Maison.

All three presets share the same section library under the hood — so you can move sections across presets, mix and match, or evolve from one starting point into something entirely your own. The preset is a starting line, not a cage.

Applying a preset

From the theme editor (recommended)

  1. Open Online Store → Themes → Customize.
  2. Click Theme settings in the top-left of the editor.
  3. Open the Theme styles picker at the top.
  4. Choose Pure, Atelier, or Maison.
  5. Save. The theme settings switch immediately; the homepage may take a refresh.
Heads up

Applying a preset overwrites your current theme settings (colors, fonts, spacing, etc.). It does not overwrite section content you've added or edited. Always duplicate your theme first if you've already done customization work.

From the Shopify CLI (developers)

If you're working with the theme locally and want to push a specific preset's homepage layout to a demo store:

# Pure — uses the base templates
shopify theme push --store=your-store.myshopify.com

# Atelier — applies listings/atelier/ overrides
shopify theme push --store=your-store.myshopify.com --listing=atelier

# Maison — applies listings/maison/ overrides
shopify theme push --store=your-store.myshopify.com --listing=maison

Mixing & customizing

A preset is the start of your design, not the end. After applying one you can:

  • Swap the heading or body font to anything in the Shopify font picker.
  • Override colors via the theme editor — your changes win over preset defaults.
  • Add sections from any preset's signature list — they all work on any preset.
  • Use both color schemes (light + dark) on the same page via per-section settings.
  • Save your changes as a custom preset by adding a new entry under config/settings_data.json → presets.
